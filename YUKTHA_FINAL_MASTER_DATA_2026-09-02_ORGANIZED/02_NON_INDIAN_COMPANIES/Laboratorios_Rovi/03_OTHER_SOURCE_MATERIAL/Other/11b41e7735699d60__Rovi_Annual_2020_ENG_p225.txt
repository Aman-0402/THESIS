32. Fees of account auditors and their group or related companies
The net fees accrued in 2020 by KPMG Auditores, S.L. for account auditing services were 196 thousand euros, who
likewise accrued 31 thousand euros for other audit-related services and 14 thousand euros for other services (157
thousand euros, 29 thousand euros and 14 thousand euros, respectively, in 2019). Services other than audit or audit-
related services included the work to review the internal control over financial reporting system and the review of
compliance with the financial ratios for financing contracts.
Additionally, the firm to which KPMG Auditores, S.L. belongs provided services for the review of the Statement of Non-
Financial Information amounting to 18 thousand euros (20 thousand euros in 2019).

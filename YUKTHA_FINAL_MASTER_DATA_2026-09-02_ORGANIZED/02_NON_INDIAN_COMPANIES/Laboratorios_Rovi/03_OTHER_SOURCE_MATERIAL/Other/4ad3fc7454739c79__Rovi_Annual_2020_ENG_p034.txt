e) Grants received
The construction of the Granada plant was partly financed by a grant awarded by the Innovation and Development
Agency of Andalusia (Innovation, Science and Enterprise Department of the Regional Government) for an amount of
5,431 thousand euros (Note 17). This grant was collected in November 2008 and the part that has not yet been allocated
to the income statement is recognised under the heading “Grants, donations and legacies received”. This grant began to
be allocated to the income statement in the second half of 2009, when depreciation of the assets for which it was granted
commenced.
f) Insurance

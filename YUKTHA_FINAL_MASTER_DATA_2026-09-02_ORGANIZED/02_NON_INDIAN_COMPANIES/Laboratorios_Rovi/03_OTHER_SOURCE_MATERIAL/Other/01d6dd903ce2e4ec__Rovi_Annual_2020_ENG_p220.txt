At 31 December, 2019, the Group held finanical derivatives of 26,500 million dollars to minimise the impact of exchange
rate risk. The measurement of these financial derivatives at fair value represented a loss of 129 thousand euros at the 31
December, 2019 reporting date. In 2020, these assets, as well as other acquired during 2020, were liquidated, incurring
a loss of 245 thousand euros on the liquidations (profit of 305 thousand euros in 2019). At 31 December, 2020, there
were active contracts of this nature of 13,500 thousand dollars, the measurement of which at the 2020 reporting date
represented a loss of 925 thousand euros.

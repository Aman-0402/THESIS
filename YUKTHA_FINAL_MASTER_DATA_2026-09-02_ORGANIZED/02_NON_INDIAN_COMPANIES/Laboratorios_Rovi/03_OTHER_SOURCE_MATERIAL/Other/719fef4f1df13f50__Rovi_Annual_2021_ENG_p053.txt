The accruals caption, both non-current and current, records the amounts received for the assignment of the rights to market
low-molecular-weight heparins in a number of countries. The Company defers the revenue over the terms of the contracts,

distribution contracts. In 2021, ROVI recognised revenue from the granting of distribution licences for a total amount of
5,140 thousand euros (944 thousand euros in 2020).

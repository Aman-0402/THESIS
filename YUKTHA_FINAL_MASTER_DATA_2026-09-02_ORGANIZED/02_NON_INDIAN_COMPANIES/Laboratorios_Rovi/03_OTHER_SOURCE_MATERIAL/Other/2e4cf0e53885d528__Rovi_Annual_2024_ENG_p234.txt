ii. Disclosure Requirement on policies and actions
This Disclosure Requirement on policies and actions required in relation to each topical standard will be disclosed under each
topical standard since the legislation requires specific policies and actions in environmental, social and governance matters.
The disclosure requirements are as follows:
• Disclosure requirement - Policies MDR-P: Policies adopted to manage material sustainability matters.
• Disclosure Requirement - Actions MDR-A: Actions and resources in relation to material sustainability matters.

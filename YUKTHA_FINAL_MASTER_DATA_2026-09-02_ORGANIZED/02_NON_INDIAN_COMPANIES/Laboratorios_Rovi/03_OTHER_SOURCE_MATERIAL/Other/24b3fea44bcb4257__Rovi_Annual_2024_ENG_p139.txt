c) Finance lease liabilities
As of 1 January 2019, as a consequence of the entry into force of IFRS Leases (Note 2.2.a), financial debt includes the lease
liabilities.
The main liabilities recognised at 31 December 2024 and 2023 under this caption related to:
– Real estate leases: the Group holds leases on certain properties where it carries out its activities. The payment period
of the liabilities generated by these leases has initially been fixed at 10 years.
– Vehicles: to carry on its activities, the Group holds leases on vehicles. The payment period for this liability is 3 years.
– Computer equipment: the Group leases certain computer equipment for its activities. The payment period established
for these liabilities is 3 years.

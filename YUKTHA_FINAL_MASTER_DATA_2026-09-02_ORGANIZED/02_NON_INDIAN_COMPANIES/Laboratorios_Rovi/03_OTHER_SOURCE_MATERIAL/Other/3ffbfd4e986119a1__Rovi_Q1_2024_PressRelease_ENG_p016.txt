DEPRECIATION
Depreciation and amortisation expenses increased by 14% to 6.6 million euros in the
first quarter of 2024, as a result of the new property, plant and equipment and intangible
asset purchases made during the last year.
NET FINANCIAL COST
Net financial cost amounted to 0.3 million euros in the first quarter of 2024, compared
to net financial income of 0.4 million euros in the first quarter of 2023. This decrease was
mainly due to (i) higher expenses related to negative exchange differences and (ii) higher
financial expenses.
EFFECTIVE TAX RATE
The effective tax rate was 21.3% in the first quarter of 2024 compared to 22.4% in the
first quarter of 2023. This decrease in the effective tax rate was due to (i) a decrease in
"Profit before income tax," and (ii) an increase in tax credits derived from research and
development expenses compared to the first quarter of 2023.

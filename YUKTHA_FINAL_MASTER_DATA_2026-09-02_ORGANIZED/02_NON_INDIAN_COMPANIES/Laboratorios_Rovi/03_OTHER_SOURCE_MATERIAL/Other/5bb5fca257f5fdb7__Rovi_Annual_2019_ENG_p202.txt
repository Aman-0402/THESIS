(Thousands of euros)
In July 2018, Enervit S.p.A. exercised a call option it held on 1% of the shares of Enervit Nutrition, S.L. for a sum of 50
thousand euros. When the option was exercised, the value at which these shares were recognised in ROVI was 40
thousand euros, generating a profit of 10 thousand euros on the transaction (Note 27). With this sale, ROVI’s percentage
interest in Enervit Nutrition, S.L. dropped from 51% to 50%.

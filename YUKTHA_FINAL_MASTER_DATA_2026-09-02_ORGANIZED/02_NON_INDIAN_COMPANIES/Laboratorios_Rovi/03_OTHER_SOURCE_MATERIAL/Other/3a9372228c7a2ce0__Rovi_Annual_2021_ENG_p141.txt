b) Debt with government entities
b.1) Since 2001, the Company has been receiving reimbursable grants from different Ministries to finance a number
of R&D projects. The amounts recorded as non-current financial debt for this item at 31 December, 2021 amounted to
8,416 thousand euros (9,119 thousand euros at 31 December, 2020). The transactions do not accrue interest and have
been recognised at their initial fair values. The difference between the initial fair value and the face value accrues at market
interest rates (Euribor and the interest rate on Spanish Treasury debt plus a spread in accordance with the Group’s risk).
This means that this debt accrues interest at effective interest rates ranging from 2.9% to 4.9%.

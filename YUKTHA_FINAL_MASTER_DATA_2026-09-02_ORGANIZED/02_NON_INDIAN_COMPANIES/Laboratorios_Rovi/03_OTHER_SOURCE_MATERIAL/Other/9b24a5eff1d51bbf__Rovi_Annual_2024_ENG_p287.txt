seeking new recovery treatment alternatives to manage this waste.

◦ Disclosure Requirement E5-6: Anticipated financial effects from material resource use
and circular economy-related risks and opportunities
Pursuant to Appendix C of ESRS 1 (List of phased-in Disclosure Requirements), this year ROVI will not disclose the
quantification of the anticipated financial effects in monetary terms of material from risks related to material resource use and
circular economy related risks, since the Company may omit this information in the first year of preparation of the Report.
Additionally, ROVI may disclose only qualitative information for the first three years of preparation. Neither will it provide a
description of the effects considered or the basic assumptions used to quantify the anticipated financial effects.

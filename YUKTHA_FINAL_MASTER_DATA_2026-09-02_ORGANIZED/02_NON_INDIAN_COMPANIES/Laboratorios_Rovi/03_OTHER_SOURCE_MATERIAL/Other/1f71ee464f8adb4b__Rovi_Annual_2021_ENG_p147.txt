a) Sales of goods
As of 31 December, 2021, the “Sales of goods” caption included 1,792 thousand euros related to services to promote third-
party products (3,179 thousand euros at 31 December, 2020).
Additionally, as of 31 December 2021, the “Sales of good” caption included 3,419 thousand euros (3,514 thousand euros
at the 2020 reporting date) related to royalties received on the basis of enoxaparin distribution agreements signed with
third parties.
Total sales of goods fell by 11,909 thousand euros in 2021 (19,393 thousand euros in 2020) as a consequence of the
rebates furnished to the National Health System (Note 2.24). 2,564 thousand euros of the total amount of rebates to the
National Health System were related to the co-operation agreement signed between Farmaindustria and the Spanish
government (6,306 thousand euros at 31 December, 2020) (Note 17).

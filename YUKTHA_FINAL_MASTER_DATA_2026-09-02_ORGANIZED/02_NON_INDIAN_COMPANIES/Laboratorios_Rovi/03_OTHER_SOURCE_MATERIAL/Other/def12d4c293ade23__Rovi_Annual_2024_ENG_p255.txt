Regarding the climate-related risk assessment, the Group has analysed its climate change management by identifying and
assessing its physical and transition risks, in addition to its opportunities. The process for determining said risks and
opportunities is described below:
1. Physical climate risks
Acute physical climate risks
ROVI’s assessment of climate-related physical risks considered the hazards described in Commission Delegated Regulation
(EU) 2021/2139 and its scope encompassed the Group’s five industrial production complexes in Spain in addition to commercial
relations with Group suppliers who are considered critical, either because they manufacture ad hoc for ROVI or because they
are unique suppliers, among other criteria. These suppliers are located in Germany, Hungary, Mexico, the United States and
China.

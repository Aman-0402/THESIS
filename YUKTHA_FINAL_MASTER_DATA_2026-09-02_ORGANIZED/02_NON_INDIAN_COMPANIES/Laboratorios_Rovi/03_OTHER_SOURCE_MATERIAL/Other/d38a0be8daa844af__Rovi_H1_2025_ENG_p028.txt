Contribution to the public health system
In Spain, according to Law 29/2006, all companies that sell prescription pharmaceuticals or other healthcare products
paid with public funds must make payments of between 1.5% and 2.0% of their sales (depending on the volume) into the
National Health System every four months. This is a levy aimed to adjust the margin on a regulated activity through the
price intervention established by the Law. The Group recognises the contribution to the public health system as a
reduction in revenue when the sale is made. The sums accrued but not yet paid are recognised under the “Other
payables” caption.
Additionally, there are liabilities in other European countries where the Group operates that have similar characteristics to
those described in the preceding paragraph and also form part of this caption.
Although these amounts should not be considered as amounts returned or reimbursed to customers, they are recognised
as a reduction in revenue, since the objective of the Law is to regulate the prices and margins obtained on these
products.

26. Seasonality
The Group’s activities have been subject to a certain degree of seasonality in the 2025 and 2024 reporting periods and
the figures for the six-month period cannot be extrapolated to the annual period. ROVI is assuming a post-pandemic
scenario in which COVID-19 is likely to be a seasonal disease and the vaccine will probably be administered once a
year. Therefore, it is expected that the Group's activity will be greater during the second half of the year.

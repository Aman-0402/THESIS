Scope 2
ROVI consumes renewable energy at practically all its work centres, including all its production centres and the Group’s main
offices, thus contributing to a decrease in the indirect emissions associated to purchasing electricity. Additionally, in 2024, self-
consumption of energy reached 6% due to the photovoltaic panels installed in previous years.

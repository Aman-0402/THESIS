3.- LIQUIDITY AND CAPITAL RESOURCES
3.1- Liquidity
As of 30 June 2025, ROVI has a gross cash position of 49.6 million euros compared to 29.3 million euros as of 31
December 2024 and net debt of 79.7 million euros (equity securities plus deposits plus financial derivatives plus cash
and cash equivalents minus current and non current financial debt), compared to 85.1 million euros as of 31 December
2024.
3.2.- Capital Resources
As of 30 June 2025, ROVI's total debt increased to 129.2 million euros. Debt with public administration represented 8%

Operating revenue increased by 54% to 648.7 million euros driven by (i) the strength of the contract manufacturing
organization (“CMO”) business, which grew by 189%, and (ii) the specialty pharmaceutical business, where sales rose
17%. Total revenue increased by 54% to 650.0 million euros in 2021.
Sales outside Spain increased by 105% to 392.0 million euros in 2021, 64.4 million euros (or 16%) of which related to
international subsidiaries, mainly due to (i) the increase in LMWH international sales and (ii) the increase in the contract
manufacturing organisation business. Sales outside Spain represented 60% of operating revenue in 2021 compared to
46% in 2020.
Sales of prescription-based pharmaceutical products rose 17% to 347.2 million euros in 2021.
Sales of the heparin franchise (Low Molecular Weight Heparins and other heparins) increased by 16% to 242.0 million
euros in 2021. Heparin sales represented 37% of operating revenue in 2021 compared to 50% in 2020.
Sales of Low Molecular Weight Heparins (LMWH) (Enoxaparin biosimilar and Bemiparin) increased by 16% to 234.8 million
euros in 2021.
Sales of the Enoxaparin biosimilar increased 22% to 124.0 million euros in 2021 mainly because of (i) the launch of the

already present. ROVI commenced the marketing of its Enoxaparin biosimilar in Germany in 2017; in UK, Italy, Spain,

lessee. These contracts include the Group’s leased
head offices in Martinsried/Planegg on the outskirts
of Munich, leased property, plant and equipment
primarily for laboratory purposes, and leased vehi-
cles for certain staff members. For information
about the capitalization of right-of-use assets, see
Note 17. Interest expenses of € 365 thousand (pre-

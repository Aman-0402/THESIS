In 2024, the Group reduced the value of its inventories by 3,857 thousand euros (3,270 thousand euros in 2023) due to
obsolescence and expiration and the measurement of the products according to the profit expected from their sale. The
reduction in value of inventories is recognised under the captions “Raw materials and consumables used” and “Change in
stocks of finished goods and work in progress” in the income statement. At 31 December 2024, the provision for the reduction of
the Group’s inventories was 21,815 thousand euros (25,672 thousand euros in 2023).
The Group did not recognise any inventories related to the performance of contracts with customers.
The inventories purchase/sale commitments for the Group at the end of the reporting period were as normal in its course of its
business. Management estimates that meeting these commitments will not generate losses for the Group. The Group holds
insurance policies to cover the risks the inventories are exposed to. The insurance cover is considered sufficient.

EBIT
EBIT decreased by 34% to 56.5 million euros in the first half of 2024, reflecting a 5.2
percentage point decline in the EBIT margin, which was down to 17.1% in the first half of
2024 from 22.4% in the first half of 2023.
NET PROFIT
Net profit decreased by 33%, from 66.6 million euros in the first half of 2023 to 44.3
million euros in the first half of 2024.

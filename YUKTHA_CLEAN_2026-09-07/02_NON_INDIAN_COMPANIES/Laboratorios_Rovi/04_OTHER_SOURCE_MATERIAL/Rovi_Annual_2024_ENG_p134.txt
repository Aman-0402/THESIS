e) Dividends
On 24 June 2024, the General Shareholders Meeting approved the application of the 2023 profit, which included a dividend to
be distributed to the shareholders for an amount of 59,618 thousand euros (1.1037 euros gross per share). The dividend was
paid out in July 2024.
On 14 June, 2023, the General Shareholders Meeting approved the application of the 2022 profit, which included a dividend to
be distributed to shareholders for a maximum total amount of 69,886 thousand euros (1.2938 euros gross per share). This
dividend was paid out in July 2023.

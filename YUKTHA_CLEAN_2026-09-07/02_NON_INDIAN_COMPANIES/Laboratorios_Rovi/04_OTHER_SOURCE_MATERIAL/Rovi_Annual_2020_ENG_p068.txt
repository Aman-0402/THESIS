32. Environmental information
Any operation the main purpose of which is to minimise the environmental impact and protect and improve the
environment is considered an environmental activity.
The Company has not made any investments in systems, equipment or facilities for environmental activities in the last
two reporting periods.
In 2020, in order to contribute to the protection and improvement of the environment, the Company incurred expenses of
715 thousand euros for waste elimination (1,192 thousand euros in 2019.
At the reporting date, the Company was not aware of any possible environmental contingencies that might be significant.

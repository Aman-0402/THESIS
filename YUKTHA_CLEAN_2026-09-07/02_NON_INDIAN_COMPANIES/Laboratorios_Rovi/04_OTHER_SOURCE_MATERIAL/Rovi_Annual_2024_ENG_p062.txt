b) Remuneration and loans to senior management
The total remuneration paid to members of senior management in 2024, excluding the remuneration received by the
executive directors described in points a)1.c and a)1.f above, was 2,349 thousand euros (1,659 thousand euros in 2023).
No loans were granted to members of senior management in the last two years.
The Company holds a liability insurance policy for directors and senior management. A premium of 205 thousand euros
accrued for this policy in 2024 (180 thousand euros in 2023).
c) Conflicts of interest on the part of the directors
In compliance with their duty to avoid situations where conflict with the Company’s interests exists, the directors who held
office on the Board of Directors during the year met the obligations set forth in article 228 of the revised text of the Capital
Companies Act. Likewise, both they and the persons related to them refrained from entering into the situations of conflict
of interest provided for in article 229 of said Act.

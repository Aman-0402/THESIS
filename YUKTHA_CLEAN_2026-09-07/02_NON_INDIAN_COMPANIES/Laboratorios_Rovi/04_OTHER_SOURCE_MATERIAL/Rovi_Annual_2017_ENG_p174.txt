In 2016, additions were recognized under the caption “Trademarks and licences” relating mainly to the acquisition of the

Total research and development expenses incurred in 2017 were 28,251 thousand euros (17,481 thousand euros in
2016) and were mainly concentrated in the Glycomics and ISM® platforms, the latter of which is a drug release system,
belonging to ROVI, the objective of which is to improve patient treament adherence. Of the total research and
development expenses incurred in 2017, 7,218 thousand euros was recognized under the “Employee expenses” (Note
23) heading (6,090 thousand euros at 31 December, 2016) and 21,033 thousand euros under “Other operating
expenses” (Note 24) (11,391 thousand euros in 2016).

5.2.3.- Liquidity risk
Management periodically monitors the liquidity estimates of the Group in accordance with the expected cash flows. The
Group maintains sufficient cash and marketable securities to meet its liquidity requirements. In 2017, ROVI signed a
financing agreement with the European Investment Bank, which it could draw down over the two years following
signature of the agreement for a total amount of 45 million euros. As of 31 December, 2019, ROVI had drawn the full
amount of this loan.
In 2020, ROVI signed credit policies for a total amount of 45 million euros. No amounts had been drawn down as of 31
December, 2020.

Notes to the Consolidated Annual Accounts for the annual period 2018
(Thousands of euros)
Additionally, 3,467 thousand euros of the total amount of rebates to the National Health System were related to the co-
operation agreement signed between Farmaindustria, the Spanish pharmaceutical industry association, and the Spanish
government in 2016 and renewed in December 2017. ROVI, as a member of Farmaindustria, is subject to this
agreement. According to the agreement, in the event that public spending on medicines (exclusing generics and
biosimilar) exceeds the actual growth rate of the GDP of the Spanish economy, the pharmaceutical industry must
reimburse the government for said excess in cash. In 2018, the public spending growth rate exceeded the growth rate of
the GDP, meaning that the companies subject to the agreement must make the applicable reimbursement (3,467
thousand euros in the case of ROVI).
Although these amounts should not be considered as returns or refunds to customers, they are recognised as a
reduction in revenue because the objective of the Law is to regulate the prices and margins obtained for these products.
In 2017, these sums were recognised under the “Provisions for other liabilties and charges” caption in the same way as
expected returns from customers. In 2018, these amounts were presented under the “Other payables” caption, like any
other refund or discount and customer returns.

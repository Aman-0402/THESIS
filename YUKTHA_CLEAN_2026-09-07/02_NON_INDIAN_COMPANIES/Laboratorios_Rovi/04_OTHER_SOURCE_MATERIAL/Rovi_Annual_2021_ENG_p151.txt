28. Earnings per share
Basic and diluted
The basic earnings per share are calculated by dividing the profit attributable to the Company’s shareholders by the
weighted average number of ordinary shares in issue during the period.
In order to determine the number of shares in issue for 2021 and 2020, the weighted average number of shares was
calculated without taking the treasury shares that existed at any given moment into account.

b) Debt with government entities
Since 2001, the Company has been receiving reimbursable grants from different Ministries to finance a number of R&D
projects. The amounts recorded as non-current financial debt for this item at 31 December, 2017 amounted to 8,746
thousand euros (9,170 thousand euros at 31 December, 2016). The transactions do not accrue interest and have been
recognized at their initial fair values. The difference between the initial fair value and the face value accrues at market
interest rates (Euribor and the interest rate on Spanish Treasury debt plus a spread in accordance with the Company’s
risk). This means that this debt accrues interest at effective interest rates ranging from 2.9% to 4.9%.

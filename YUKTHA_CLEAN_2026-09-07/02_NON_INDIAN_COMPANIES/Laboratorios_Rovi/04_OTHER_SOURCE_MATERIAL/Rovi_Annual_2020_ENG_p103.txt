Article 17 of ROVI’s Regulations of the Board of Directors, which incorporates Recommendation 25 of the Good Governance Code, limits to
ten the number of company boards of which its directors may form part (and a limit of eight if they are companies whose shares a traded
on Spanish or foreign stock exchanges). The latter excludes positions that directors may hold in certain cases and a provision is made for
the possibility that, depending on the circumstances of each case, the Appointments and Remuneration Committee may expressly authorise
the director otherwise.

On June 26, 2025, the German Bundestag passed
the “Act on an Immediate Tax Investment Pro-gram
to Strengthen Germany as a Business Loca-tion,”
which will introduce a gradual reduction in the cor-
porate income tax rate of one percentage point per
year starting in 2028. The tax rate will thus fall from
the current 15% to 10% by 2032 (Section 23 (1)
KStG, new version). The Bundesrat approved the
law on July 11, 2025. Deferred tax assets and liabili-
ties are to be measured in accord-ance with IAS
12.47 using the expected tax rates at the time the
asset is realized or the liability is settled. The reval-
uation of deferred tax assets and liabilities results
in deferred tax income of € 13,811 thousand in the
2025 financial year, which is reflected in the recon-
ciliation of expected income tax expense under the
line item “Effect of tax rates”.

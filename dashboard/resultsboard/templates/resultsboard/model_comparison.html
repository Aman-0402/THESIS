How to read these metrics

  • Accuracy — fraction of test quarters correctly classified.
  • Precision — of quarters predicted "up", how many actually were.
  • Recall — of quarters that actually went "up", how many were caught.
  • Specificity — of quarters that actually went "down", how many were caught.
  • F1 — harmonic mean of precision and recall.
  • ROC-AUC — ranking quality across all thresholds; 0.5 = random, 1.0 = perfect.
  • Validation accuracy — accuracy on the 2022–2023 validation slice, shown for reference only (not used to pick a "winner").
